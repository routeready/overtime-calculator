"Mornin', Hank. Boat's gassed, cooler's loaded, here's yer bait. You owe me $250 by sundown for the rental — and don't think I forgot last week's tab neither. Oh, and watch the lake patrol… they been crackin' down on day-drinkers."

Catch fish, sell 'em to Earl, and pay your tab before the sun goes down. Pay up and you live to fish another day — each one a little harder than the last. Miss it and Earl takes the boat.
