## 🎣 Bass & Brews — A Day on Loon Lake (mini-game)

An arcade fishing game lives in [`fishing.html`](fishing.html). You're Hank,
and Earl fronted you the boat, the bait, and a cooler of beer. Now you owe him
a **tab by sundown** — pay it and you fish another day, each one harder than
the last; miss it and Earl repossesses the boat.

- **W A S D / Arrows** — drive the boat
- **Space** — cast, then set the hook & reel when a fish bites
- **B** — crack a beer (raises your buzz 🍺)
- **F** — flee from the cops

**The story loop**
- **Daylight clock** — every day has a countdown to sundown when Earl's tab comes due.
- **Beer & the law** — drinking builds your *buzz*, which draws the lake patrol.
  Comply for a fine, or hit the throttle. Each successful escape grows your
  *reputation* — and the cops bring faster boats and try harder next time.
- **Story events** — radio chatter, schooling fish, storms, friendly beers, and
  patrol crackdowns roll in as you fish.
- **Old Mossback** 🐋 — the legendary musky surfaces at a green-glowing spot.
  Win the multi-stage fight to land him for a huge payout and a permanent title.
- **Endings** — pay your way to a higher rep, get arrested fleeing the law, or
  go broke at sundown. Multiple outcomes to chase.

Touch controls are supported on mobile.
