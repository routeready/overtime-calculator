## 🎣 Bass & Brews (mini-game)

A little arcade fishing game lives in [`fishing.html`](fishing.html). Boat
around the lake, park over a dark fishing hole, and cast to reel in fish.

- **W A S D / Arrows** — drive the boat
- **Space** — cast / set the hook & reel
- **B** — crack a beer (raises your buzz 🍺)
- **F** — flee from the cops

Drink too much and the lake patrol shows up. Pull over for a fine, or hit the
throttle and run — but if they catch you, it's the slammer. Touch controls are
supported on mobile.
